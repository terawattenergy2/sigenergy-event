# Voucher background

File: `public/assets/voucher-background.jpg`
Created using the built-in ImageGen tool (concept art, not an official product photograph).

Final prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset: background for a blue event voucher themed around Sigenergy inverter and solar energy storage. Landscape 1536x1024. Premium photorealistic 3D studio scene, white modular home battery energy storage tower with compact inverter controller at its top, elegant rounded enclosure and subtle thin dark ventilation details, inspired by the visual category of Sigenergy SigenStor, with solar panels glimpsed in the far background. Put the equipment on the RIGHT third, leaving the LEFT two thirds clean dark midnight-blue negative space for readable voucher typography added separately. Electric blue and cyan rim lights, deep navy background, soft atmospheric light, restrained technical product photography. No words, no text, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no borders. This is conceptual energy equipment imagery, not an exact official product photograph.

Font: Noto Sans Thai from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansthai — SIL Open Font License, saved in `public/fonts/OFL.txt`.

Voucher text, numbers, personal data and QR codes are rendered separately by the application. The background contains no personal information.

Voucher text uses Kanit Regular/Bold as a visual approximation of the supplied Canva Sans reference. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kanit. License: public/fonts/Kanit-OFL.txt (SIL Open Font License). Fonts are bundled for server rendering on Vercel.
